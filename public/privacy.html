Privacy

MESSED UP - last updated 8 October 2026

Playing without an account

Practice mode needs no sign-in. Your settings, practice best score and achievements are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is only needed for the ranked Daily Run, streaks and the leaderboard. When you sign in, Google tells us a numeric account identifier. We do not receive or store your email address, real name or photo.

We store, linked to that identifier:

  • your nickname (shown on the leaderboard; you choose it and can change it),
  • your Daily Run results: date, difficulty, verified score and courses cleared,
  • your streak and best score.

To check a score, the game sends the moves you made during the run; the server replays them and keeps only the resulting score.

Streak reminders

If you turn on reminders in Settings, your browser gives us a push address for this device. We use it only to send one evening reminder when your streak is about to end, and delete it when you turn reminders off, sign out of this device, delete your account, or the address stops working.

Error reports

If the game crashes, your browser may send us the error message and the place in our code where it happened, so we can fix it. No personal data is included. Recent reports are kept for a short time and then overwritten.

Cookies

One cookie, mu_s, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is not used for tracking. Google's sign-in button may set its own cookies, covered by Google's privacy policy.

Who processes the data

The game is hosted on Vercel; account and leaderboard data is stored in Upstash Redis. There are no ads and no analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

Open Settings > Account > Delete account in the game. It removes your nickname, streak, every score and your reminder subscriptions straight away. (A note that today's Daily Run was used is kept until it expires after a few days, so the attempt cannot be replayed.) Encrypted weekly backups are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. You can also ask on the project's GitHub page.

Sign out everywhere in the same place ends your session on every device.
